## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms



The cloud account management and FinOps market has consolidated around platforms that charge as a percentage of managed cloud spend, creating pricing that scales with your infrastructure. AWS Account Management and Azure Cost Management are **free native tools** included with cloud subscriptions, while third-party platforms like Cloudability and CloudHealth charge based on managed spend bands, with overage fees that can reach **$4,410 per unit** in Premium tiers . Spot by NetApp has a **median buyer cost of $109,384/year**, and Turbonomic starts at **$225/year for Standard** or a percentage of cloud spend .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[OpenCost](https://github.com/opencost/opencost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/opencost/opencost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/opencost/opencost/stargazers)  

  **The open-source cost monitoring tool for Kubernetes and cloud spend**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Originally developed and open-sourced by Kubecost** . Real-time cost allocation by cluster, node, namespace, controller, service, or pod. **Multi-cloud monitoring for AWS, Azure, GCP** with dynamic on-demand pricing from cloud billing APIs. Supports on-prem clusters with custom CSV pricing. **MCP server built into Helm chart** (opt-in, port 8081) for AI agent access to allocation, asset, and cloud cost queries. **Carbon costs** for cloud resources. Free and open-source distribution . 🎯



- **[Kubecost](https://github.com/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/kubecost/cost-analyzer-helm-chart/stargazers)  

  **Kubernetes cost monitoring and optimization**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **The commercial successor to OpenCost** — Kubecost provides the full-featured platform while OpenCost remains the open-source core. Real-time cost visibility, savings recommendations, and multi-cluster cost allocation. **Free tier available** for single-cluster deployments. 📊



- **[FinOps Foundation Landscape](https://github.com/finopsfoundation/foundation)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/finopsfoundation/foundation?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/finopsfoundation/foundation/stargazers)  

  **Community-driven FinOps framework and best practices**, open-source. **13 stars** . **FinOps Landscape** (58 stars) maps the entire ecosystem of cloud financial management tools. **The definitive reference for FinOps practitioners** seeking to understand the tooling landscape . 🗺️



- **[Infracost](https://github.com/infracost/infracost)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/infracost/infracost?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/infracost/infracost/stargazers)  

  **Cloud cost estimates for Terraform in pull requests**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~10k+ stars**. Shows cost impact of infrastructure changes before deployment. Supports AWS, Azure, GCP, and 1,000+ resources. **Shift-left FinOps** — catch cost regressions in CI/CD. CLI and GitHub/GitLab integration. 💰



- **[Cloud Custodian](https://github.com/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cloud-custodian/cloud-custodian/stargazers)  

  **Rules engine for cloud security, cost optimization, and governance**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~5k+ stars**. YAML-based policies for AWS, Azure, GCP. **Automated remediation** — tag enforcement, idle resource cleanup, rightsizing, and compliance. **The most mature open-source cloud governance engine**. 🛡️



- **[Komiser](https://github.com/tailwarden/komiser)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/tailwarden/komiser?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/tailwarden/komiser/stargazers)  

  **Cloud resource visibility and cost optimization**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~4k+ stars**. **Multi-cloud dashboard** for AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean, and Kubernetes. Detects idle resources, orphaned volumes, and untagged assets. **Self-hosted or Komiser Cloud**. 🔍



- **[CloudQuery](https://github.com/cloudquery/cloudquery)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cloudquery/cloudquery?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cloudquery/cloudquery/stargazers)  

  **Cloud asset inventory and CSPM**, MPL-2.0 licensed. **~5k+ stars**. **Extracts cloud configuration into PostgreSQL** for querying and analysis. **Enables custom FinOps queries** across multi-cloud environments. The foundation for building custom cost governance dashboards. 📦



- **[Steampipe](https://github.com/turbot/steampipe)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/turbot/steampipe?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/turbot/steampipe/stargazers)  

  **Query cloud APIs with SQL**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **~7k+ stars**. **Zero-ETL approach** — query AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, and 100+ services directly. **Build custom cost reports with SQL**. The simplest way to explore cloud billing data without data pipelines. 🔗



- **[Cloud Carbon Footprint](https://github.com/cloud-carbon-footprint/cloud-carbon-footprint)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cloud-carbon-footprint/cloud-carbon-footprint?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cloud-carbon-footprint/cloud-carbon-footprint/stargazers)  

  **Estimate and visualize cloud carbon emissions**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~1k+ stars**. **Green FinOps** — correlates cloud spend with carbon impact. Supports AWS, Azure, GCP. **The leading open-source tool for cloud sustainability**. 🌱



- **[AWS Cost Explorer CLI](https://github.com/aws/aws-cost-explorer-cli)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/aws/aws-cost-explorer-cli?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/aws/aws-cost-explorer-cli/stargazers)  

  **CLI for AWS Cost Explorer**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Query cost and usage data from terminal**. Export reports, filter by service/region/tag, and integrate with scripts. **Lightweight alternative to AWS Console** for FinOps engineers. 🖥️



- **[Azure Cost CLI](https://github.com/mivano/azure-cost-cli)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/mivano/azure-cost-cli?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/mivano/azure-cost-cli/stargazers)  

  **CLI for Azure Cost Management**, MIT licensed. **Query Azure costs from terminal**. Daily/monthly cost breakdowns, service-level analysis, and budget tracking. **Simple, scriptable Azure FinOps**. 🔵



- **[GCP Cost CLI](https://github.com/...)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **CLI for Google Cloud Billing**, open-source. **Query GCP costs from terminal**. Project-level and service-level breakdowns. **Lightweight GCP FinOps tooling**. 🌐



- **[Turbonomic Alternatives (Open-Source)](https://github.com/...)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **Open-source alternatives to Turbonomic**, open-source. **Kubernetes rightsizing and resource optimization**. Tools like **Goldilocks** (VPA recommendations), **KRR** (Kubernetes Resource Recommender), and **Popeye** (cluster sanitization) provide free alternatives to commercial APM-driven optimization . ⚖️

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Cloudability overage fees** range from **$1,930 (Essentials) to $4,410 (Premium) per unit** above contracted spend — a 20% spend growth mid-year can add tens of thousands in unplanned fees . **Percentage-of-spend pricing couples your tooling cost to your cloud bill** — costs rise even when feature usage stays flat .

- **CloudHealth pricing is quote-based** — no public list price. The platform tells you what to do but **doesn't automate optimization**; rightsizing, commitment purchases, and anomaly response remain manual work .

- **Turbonomic's $225/year Standard tier** is for on-premises datacenter optimization; public cloud optimization requires connecting AWS, Azure, and GCP accounts and is priced as a **percentage of cloud spend** .

- Open-source solutions (OpenCost, Infracost, Cloud Custodian) provide self-hosted ownership and transparency, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees, multi-cloud cost allocation at scale, and vendor support remain primarily commercial offerings. **Always validate cost data against native cloud billing consoles** before making financial decisions. 💰
